About Us

Memphis Deep is an asset protection and improvement partner organization headquartered and operating in Memphis, Tennesse, with short term plans to expand to connected cities like Chicago, New Orlelans, and beyond. Build on Trust and Led By Faith, we are your partner for commercial and residental real estate services (deep cleaning, condition reporting, and repairs/improvements), throughout the asset's lifecycle.

With nearly two decades of experience, our goal is simple. We exist to educate and help others protect their assets, while improving the American stanard of living, at scale, through a Carpenters That Clean model.
